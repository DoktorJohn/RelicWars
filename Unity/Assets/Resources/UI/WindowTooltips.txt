# Window tooltips
#
# Edit the text below each heading. The heading must match a WindowTypeEnum name
# and must keep the "## " prefix. Blank lines are allowed inside a tooltip.

## TownHall
Manage your city's construction queue and choose which buildings to construct or upgrade. The Town Hall is the central overview for expanding the city.

## Barracks
Recruit infantry units and review their costs, training time, requirements, and combat statistics.

## Warehouse
Review the city's storage capacity. Upgrading the warehouse increases how many standard resources the city can hold.

## TimberCamp
Review and upgrade the city's wood production. Higher levels increase the amount of wood produced over time.

## StoneQuarry
Review and upgrade the city's stone production. Higher levels increase the amount of stone produced over time.

## MetalMine
Review and upgrade the city's metal production. Higher levels increase the amount of metal produced over time.

## Housing
Review and upgrade the city's housing. Housing determines the population capacity available for the city's development.

## Wall
Review and upgrade the city wall. Stronger walls improve the city's defensive capabilities.

## Stable
Recruit mounted units and review their costs, training time, requirements, and combat statistics.

## Harbor
Recruit naval units, including transport ships, and review their costs, training time, capacity, and combat statistics.

## Workshop
Craft equipment and manage workshop activities that improve your military capabilities.

## University
Review the university and its upgrades. The university supports research and unlocks further development.

## MarketPlace
Trade resources through the marketplace and review the offers currently available to your city.

## Profile
Review a player's public profile, including their identity and available world-related information.

## Alliance
Review alliance information, members, invitations, and the alliance actions available to your role.

## Rankings
Compare players and alliances by their current ranking. Select a name to open the related profile or alliance.

## Overview
See an overview of your cities and open a city to manage it directly.

## Research
Choose and start research that unlocks new capabilities. Locked research shows the requirements that must be completed first.

## Message
Read conversations, send messages to other players, and create or delete conversations.

## IdeologyFocus
Choose and manage ideology focuses that provide bonuses and shape your empire's development.

## Hexagon
Inspect the selected world-map location and its city information. Available actions depend on ownership and diplomatic relations.

## UnitDeployment
Select units and issue an available deployment order. Travel time and valid actions depend on the selected target.

## CombatSimulator
Preview a battle outcome by configuring attacker and defender unit stacks before committing to a real deployment.

## Reports
Review battle and deployment reports. Select a report to inspect its participants, outcome, losses, and other details.

## Island
Review the selected island, its resource opportunities, and the investments available to your empire.

## ExoticResource
Review your exotic resources and how they contribute to island investments and other advanced activities.

## BugReport
Send a bug report to the development team. Include what happened and enough detail to reproduce the problem.

## Dailies
Review your 20 daily objectives, current progress, reward tiers, and countdown to the next UTC reset. Coming Soon objectives cannot progress; rewards and claiming are not active yet.

## Administration
Review your active troop deployments across every city, including mission phase, route, troop manifest, and live timing.
